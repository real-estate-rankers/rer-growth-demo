About Goose Creek

Getting to Know Goose Creek

Goose Creek incorporated its first section in 1961 and has grown into the largest city in Berkeley County. It covers about 42 square miles, with Naval Weapons Station Charleston next door along the Cooper River.

Most homes in the city are single-family houses. Buyers often compare Goose Creek with Summerville and North Charleston when they want more space for their budget.

Real Estate

Homes in Goose Creek

About three in four homes in Goose Creek are single-family detached houses, according to the city's comprehensive plan. Neighborhoods range from established golf communities to newer subdivisions.

Commutes depend a lot on where you land, since the city spreads out along US-52 and US-176. We can show you how each neighborhood connects to the main roads.

Berkeley County Taxes

Goose Creek homes are in Berkeley County, so the 4% legal residence application goes to Berkeley County, not Charleston County. We remind every buyer after closing.

Goose Creek FAQs

Which school district serves Goose Creek?

Berkeley County School District serves Goose Creek. Confirm the assigned schools for a specific address with the district.

Is Goose Creek in Berkeley County?

Yes. It is the largest city in Berkeley County.

How do you get to I-26 from Goose Creek?

The city connects to I-26 at several exits, using US-52 (Goose Creek Boulevard) and US-176 (St. James Avenue).

What is Crowfield?

An established neighborhood built around the Crowfield Golf and Country Club, on land that began as a plantation grant in 1701.
