Seller FAQs

Common Questions From Sellers

How do you price my home?

We start with recent sales of similar homes nearby, then adjust for condition and the homes currently on the market.

Should I make repairs before listing?

Some, not all. We walk through the home with you and point out the updates that tend to pay off and the ones that usually do not.

How long will it take to sell?

It depends mostly on price and condition. We share recent days on market for homes like yours during the walkthrough.

Who handles the closing?

A South Carolina attorney conducts the closing. Taylor keeps the attorney and the buyer's lender on schedule.
