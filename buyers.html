Buyer FAQs

Common Questions From Buyers

Do I need an attorney to buy a home in South Carolina?

Yes. South Carolina closings are conducted under the supervision of a licensed South Carolina attorney. Our post on attorney closings explains what that looks like.

What is the 4% assessment ratio?

If the home will be your legal residence, you can apply to have it taxed at the 4% owner-occupied ratio instead of 6%. It is not automatic, so apply after you close.

Which city is right for me?

It depends on your commute and your budget. Our city guides are a good place to start, and we are happy to talk it through.

Can you help me buy from out of state?

Yes. We tour by live video and coordinate a remote closing with the attorney when needed.
