Questions

What do I need besides the $999?

Hosting, and your first two months are included. After that it is $30 a month. We set it up and connect everything before launch, and you can add Care if you want us to handle updates and edits. If you do not have a domain yet, we register one in your name and the first year is included.

What counts as a city or neighborhood page?

Any area you want to be known for. It can be a city, a town, a neighborhood or a community. You choose up to five on your details form, and we write each page for that area.

Do you write the content for me?

Yes. Every page is written for your market using the details form you fill out, just like the demo site. You can ask for changes during your revision round.

Do you write blog posts too?

The Growth build sets up your blog and post layout so you can publish right away. Ongoing blog writing is part of our SEO packages, so if you want us to write your posts, that is the place to start.

Can I show listings on a Growth site?

Listing search is not part of the Growth build. If your brokerage already gives you a listing search page, we can link to it from each city page. If you want IDX built in, see our larger website builds.

Does it work for a solo agent?

Yes. The demo shows a team, but the Team page becomes an About page for a solo agent. Everything else works the same way.

Do I really own the website?

Yes. Your domain is registered in your name, and once the build is paid, the website files and content belong to you. While you are with us, the site runs on our hosting so we can keep it updated and backed up. If you ever leave, we move it to hosting in your name or send you a complete copy, at no charge.

How long does it take?

Two weeks from the day we receive all of your details, including your photos, bios, area list and domain access. The sooner those come in, the sooner you launch.

Can I add more pages later?

Yes. Your Growth site is built so we can add more area pages or IDX search later. Tell us what you want to add and we will quote it.

Can I talk to someone before I sign up?

Of course. Book a short call and we will answer anything you want to know first.
