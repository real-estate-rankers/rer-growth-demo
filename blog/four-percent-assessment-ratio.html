The 4% Assessment Ratio: Apply After You Close

By Morgan Hartwell · October 2026

Congratulations on your new home. Before you unpack every box, there is one property tax step worth doing right away.

4% Versus 6%

Under South Carolina Code Section 12-43-220, a legal residence and up to five contiguous acres can be assessed at 4% of fair market value. Most other real property is assessed at 6%. On the same home, that difference adds up quickly.

It Is Not Automatic

The county will not switch you to 4% on its own. You have to apply, and you need to qualify, which generally means the home is your legal residence. Apply as soon as you close, no later than the deadline on the county's form.

Charleston County

Charleston County's application requires an original signature. You can apply by mail or in person at the Assessor's Office, 4050 Bridge View Drive in North Charleston, (843) 958-4100. The county lists the deadline on its form, typically January 15.

Berkeley and Dorchester Counties

If you bought in Goose Creek, or in parts of North Charleston or Summerville, your home may be in Berkeley or Dorchester County instead. Berkeley County takes applications through an online portal. Check your county assessor's website for the current form and deadline.

We remind every buyer about this after closing. If you are not sure which county your home is in, ask us.

This post is general information, not legal or tax advice. Rules and deadlines can change, so confirm the details with your attorney or county office.
